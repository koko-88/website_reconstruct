# Direction grounded in images and commercial intent

Use the project's existing design artifact; do not create parallel governance. Capture the
visitor's job, primary action, proof needed, content, identity constraints, actual assets,
target devices and delivery/performance budget. For a small edit, reuse approved direction.
For a substantial new concept, present a small set of genuinely different directions before
expensive production. Ask only for missing decisions, not a fixed interview ritual.

## Moodboard to visual contract

Build a compact board of actual approved images/captures: composition, type, material/light and
one motion reference if relevant. Sources can come from another industry. Record source/link,
rights/reuse status and the specific lesson: e.g. generous subject scale, offset type rhythm,
soft contact light. Say what will differ in our work. A reference image is inspiration, not a
license or a recovered implementation. Send reverse-reference inspection to its owning skill.

Translate the board into decisions someone can inspect: subject/background relationship,
foreground/middle/background depth when useful, type scale/line breaks, contrast, crop/focal
points, palette/material roles and one signature compositional rule. Include representative
desktop and mobile frames with real content. Use original source assets or clearly labeled
provisional ones. Label an unviewed link unviewed; do not invent a visual observation.

For image-led work, derive color and atmosphere from the actual asset family; inspect the images
before choosing the palette. Use installed UI UX Pro Max design-system search for useful
alternatives and accessibility/stack advice. Its suggestions are hypotheses, not mandatory
fonts/styles. Frontend Design helps resist generic habits; a banned-looking motif may be the
right choice for this brand. Existing project typography and HBC contracts outrank both.

## Structural uniqueness and the signature

Describe why a visitor would remember this particular product. Examine layout silhouette,
reading sequence, subject behavior, visual grammar, interaction and copy as well as palette.
Test: remove the logo and accent color; is the experience still specific? A signature can be
a tactile product choice, meaningful reveal, artful typographic composition or a quiet proof
interaction. Do not require an unfamiliar device in every section or confuse noise with novelty.
Compare available approved references and sibling projects when relevant; do not create a global
fingerprint database or assert uniqueness across websites you have not inspected.

## Mobile is a composition

Specify a focal crop, subject scale, line breaks, content order, touch alternative and useful
reading duration. Replace impossible wide choreography with a deliberate compact beat/manual
step/static composition. Do not merely shrink desktop or hide essential text. Check narrow,
medium and wide aspect ratios, browser chrome/safe areas and orientation changes. A low-quality
profile should retain identity and meaningful content. Choose fonts with licensed files,
fallback metrics and realistic loading budgets; offline workbench fonts are not a factory style.

## Human decision

Present a concrete composition/moodboard with reasons, provisional assets and open questions.
Record who approved which direction and what remains unresolved in the owning artifact. Approval
of a palette alone does not approve a different storyboard or asset identity. Proceed with
already-approved and reversible engineering; stop only work dependent on a missing creative
decision. Final visual acceptance is a separate human decision after browser review.
