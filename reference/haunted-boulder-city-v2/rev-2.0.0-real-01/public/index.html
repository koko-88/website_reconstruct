BEFORE
YOU
HEAD OUT.

Find your tour
What’s the walk like?

About an hour and less than half a mile, on level public sidewalks. An easy pace, with plenty of time for stories.

Can the whole family come?

Yes! All ages are welcome on the tour and at Beer Zombies Boulder City. You don’t have to be a beer drinker to join the fun.

How scary is it?

Expect spooky stories and an eerie atmosphere, with no jump scares. The tour explores ghosts, local legends, and darker moments in Boulder City’s history. Parents can use their judgment about how comfortable their children are with those subjects.

Can I bring my dog?

Absolutely. Well-behaved dogs are welcome at Beer Zombies Boulder City and on the tour. Bring your four-legged ghost hunter along for the adventure.

Can I drink a beer on the tour?

It's perfectly legal to drink a beer while you take our walking tour!

Are wheelchairs and strollers welcome?

Yes. Wheelchairs and strollers can participate in the tour. The route follows level public sidewalks for less than half a mile, with an easy pace and stops along the way for stories.

When should I arrive?

Please arrive at least 10 minutes before your scheduled tour. Meet inside Beer Zombies at 567 Nevada Way, or outside if the venue is closed. Most downtown parking is free.

Late arrivals are not eligible for a refund.

Can I take photos?

Photos are welcome. Audio and video recording are not permitted.
