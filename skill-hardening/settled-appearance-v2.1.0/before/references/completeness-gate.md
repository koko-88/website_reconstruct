# Separate pre-implementation gates

Assess the named contract/package revision and exact route/state/environment scope. Use PASS, BLOCKED, NOT ASSESSED, or NOT REQUIRED with a scoped reason. No completion percentage or screenshot count grants clearance.

| Gate | PASS requires | Does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence-package integrity | Actual files open/parse, expected size/hash/link checks, unique IDs, original/derived separation, manifest closure, capture source/environment/state/time/readiness and portable replay/provenance. Failed saves are absent evidence. A checksum cannot authenticate a source by itself. | Fidelity, rights, completeness of live behavior, accepted implementation |
| Fidelity readiness | Every exact scoped obligation has trustworthy appearance/rules/state/temporal evidence and replay; mandatory reports/matrices populated; source conflicts resolved; no material unknown affecting those obligations. Required inference is explicit and contract-bounded with an acceptance check. | Adaptation/data readiness or reuse permission |
| Adaptation readiness | Real target content/action authorities, slot mapping/cardinality/overflow, replacement boundaries, relevant stress fixtures, invariant rules and intentional deviations documented. Reference-equivalent validation precedes actual integration. For reference-only scope use NOT REQUIRED. | Tested integration or a completed application |
| Asset/reuse decisions | For each needed asset/code/font, record authorized source/license/owner instruction, acquisition method, intended use and attribution constraints, or authorized replacement with its fidelity effect. Public availability/maps do not confer reuse rights. Out-of-scope assets may be NOT REQUIRED with a reason. | A general legal opinion or blanket permission |

Rights uncertainty does not corrupt an intact evidence package. An intact package can correctly have BLOCKED fidelity or reuse gates. Reuse established decisions; no automatic requirement for the user to sign every gate. Record missing decisions and responsible owner rather than research unrelated legal questions.

## Coverage and uncertainty

Rows use Complete, Incomplete, N/A (inspected absence with evidence), or Excluded (actual scope authority). Include all unique route/state transitions and combinations that change layout/behavior; avoid a blind Cartesian product by documenting proven equivalent groups. A missing capability is unknown, never N/A.

Classify impact **against contract obligations**: critical for wrong source/untrustworthy baseline/core journey; high for material layout/font/asset/breakpoint/interaction/rendering uncertainty; low for bounded detail with little structural effect. Confidence is a separate evidence-based judgment. Do not down-rank a required core state because it is hard to inspect.

For every retained unknown/inference: affected contract/coverage IDs, impact and basis, blocking gate or explicit no-impact reason, bounded rule/check, owner and reopen trigger. Unrelated service internals need not block an authorized replacement, but remain unknown. Static-only sources can clear static appearance scope; they cannot establish unobserved motion/responsiveness/behavior.

## Clearance record

Record gate, result, assessed revision/time/assessor, supporting rows/artifacts, incomplete obligations, residual limits and smallest resolving action. Then explicitly derive:

- **Reconstruction cleared** only for integrity PASS + fidelity PASS + required asset/reuse PASS.
- **Adaptation cleared** only when the above and adaptation readiness PASS; completed integration is a later result.
- **Evidence-only delivery complete** when requested inspection, reports and honest assessment are delivered, including unavailable coverage and downstream BLOCKED results.

Independently requested complete scope may clear separately only when shared dependencies are covered. Never convert blocked whole-scope work into unrequested partial implementation. New source/version/scope, changed material artifacts or discovered rules reopen affected gates/rows. Frozen decisions stay in the old revision.
